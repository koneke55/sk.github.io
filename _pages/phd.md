## Research vision

I want to advance **learning-based perception and control** on **real hardware constraints**: limited power, latency budgets, and noisy sensors. My prior work spans **published BMS deep learning**, **medical image analysis** (diabetic retinopathy cotton-wool detection), **speaker recognition with neural nets**, and ongoing **multimodal / computer vision** research aimed at top-tier venues.

I am especially motivated by problems where **theory meets deployment**: co-design of models and embedded platforms (MCU, FPGA, VLSI), not only offline accuracy benchmarks.

## Primary research interests

1. **Computer vision & multimodal learning** — ViTs, vision-language models, medical and industrial imaging  
2. **Reinforcement learning & AI robotics** — autonomous agents, human-robot interaction, sim-to-real  
3. **Embedded AI & edge ML** — TinyML, real-time inference, hardware–software co-design  
4. **Deep learning for signal processing** — BMS, speech, biomedical and RF-aware sensing  
5. **Graph & quantum ML** — structured data and emerging compute paradigms (exploratory)
