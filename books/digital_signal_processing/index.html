Digital Signal Processing

P. Ramesh Babu  ·  2025

2023-06-01  ·  2023-08-31  ·   · 

2023  ·  technical-reading   dsp   digital-signal-processing  ·  engineering   signal-processing   textbook


A comprehensive textbook on Digital Signal Processing designed to meet the syllabi requirements of undergraduate engineering courses across all circuit branches. The treatment of the subject is simplified for thorough understanding, covering fundamental concepts, signal analysis, filtering techniques, and practical applications.

Published by Vijay Nicole Imprints Pvt Ltd (2025 Edition), this book provides excellent coverage of DSP fundamentals essential for Electronics & Communication Engineering students. The simplified approach makes complex DSP concepts accessible while maintaining technical rigor.

Key topics include discrete-time signals and systems, z-transforms, DFT/FFT algorithms, digital filter design, and multirate signal processing. An essential resource for mastering Digital Signal Processing concepts.
